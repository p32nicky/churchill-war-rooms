Getting There

Walking to the Churchill War Rooms from the River Thames

A scenic riverside walk to the Churchill War Rooms.

The Churchill War Rooms, a fascinating museum showcasing Winston Churchill's leadership during World War II, can be easily accessed on foot from the River Thames, offering a scenic and historic walk to this iconic London landmark.

Starting Your Journey

Your walk to the Churchill War Rooms begins along the South Bank of the River Thames, where you can take in the stunning views of the river and the city's skyline. From here, you can follow the pedestrian path westwards, passing by several notable landmarks, including the London Eye and the Westminster Bridge.

Navigating to Whitehall

As you continue your walk, you will eventually reach Westminster Bridge, which you can cross to reach the north bank of the Thames. Once across, turn right and follow the road, which will take you to Whitehall, a street lined with historic buildings and monuments. To help guide you, look out for the following landmarks:

  • Parliament Square, home to the Houses of Parliament and Big Ben
  • The Cenotaph, a war memorial honouring Britain's war dead
  • Horse Guards Parade, a historic parade ground and venue for the annual Trooping the Colour ceremony

Arriving at the Churchill War Rooms

After a short walk along Whitehall, you will arrive at the Churchill War Rooms, located beneath the streets of Whitehall. This underground bunker served as the British government's command centre during World War II and features a museum showcasing the life and legacy of Winston Churchill. Be sure to check the attraction's website to confirm opening times and plan your visit accordingly.

Adding Context to Your Visit

To add an extra layer of depth and understanding to your visit, consider joining a guided Westminster WWII walking tour. These tours provide a wealth of information about the area's history and significance during the war, and can help you appreciate the importance of the Churchill War Rooms and their role in the war effort.

Practical Information

Before embarking on your walk to the Churchill War Rooms, it's a good idea to plan your journey and check for any road closures or disruptions. You may also want to consider purchasing a map or guidebook to help navigate the area. Additionally, be sure to confirm the Churchill War Rooms' admission procedures and ticket availability at checkout to avoid any delays or disappointment.
