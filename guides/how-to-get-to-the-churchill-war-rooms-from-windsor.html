Getting There

How to Get to the Churchill War Rooms from Windsor

The quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Windsor by Tube, train, bus, or on foot.

The Churchill War Rooms, a museum showcasing Winston Churchill's leadership during World War II, is a must-visit attraction in London. Located in the heart of the city, it is easily accessible from various points, including Windsor. If you are planning to visit the Churchill War Rooms from Windsor, there are several transportation options available, each with its own advantages.

Introduction to Routes from Windsor

Windsor, a town famous for its castle and historic architecture, is situated approximately 25 miles west of central London. The journey to the Churchill War Rooms from Windsor can be made by Tube, train, bus, or on foot, depending on your preference and the time you have available. It is essential to plan your route in advance and consider factors such as travel time, cost, and convenience.

Travel by Train

Taking the train is one of the quickest ways to travel from Windsor to London. You can take a train from Windsor & Eton Central railway station to London Paddington station, then take the Tube (Bakerloo line) from Paddington to Westminster station, which is a short walk from the Churchill War Rooms. Alternatively, you can take a train from Windsor & Eton Riverside station to London Waterloo station, and from there, take the Tube (Jubilee line) to Westminster station.

Travel by Tube

Although there is no direct Tube line from Windsor to the Churchill War Rooms, you can take a train to a London station and then use the Tube to complete your journey. The nearest Tube station to the Churchill War Rooms is Westminster, which is served by the Jubilee, District, and Circle lines. From Westminster station, exit the Tube and walk west on Bridge Street towards Parliament Square, then turn left onto King Charles Street to reach the Churchill War Rooms.

  • Take the Jubilee line from London Bridge or other stations to Westminster
  • Take the District or Circle line from Victoria or other stations to Westminster
  • Follow the signs from Westminster station to the Churchill War Rooms

Travel by Bus

Taking the bus is another option for travelling from Windsor to the Churchill War Rooms. You can take a bus from Windsor town centre to London Victoria Coach Station, then take a short bus ride or taxi to the Churchill War Rooms. This option may take longer than the train or Tube, but it can be a more affordable choice.

Walking and Guided Tours

While walking from Windsor to the Churchill War Rooms is not a practical option due to the distance, you can take a guided Westminster WWII walking tour once you arrive in London. This tour provides an excellent way to add context to your visit to the Churchill War Rooms, as you will learn more about Winston Churchill's leadership and the impact of World War II on the city. The walking tour will take you through historic London, pointing out significant landmarks and sharing stories about the war effort.
