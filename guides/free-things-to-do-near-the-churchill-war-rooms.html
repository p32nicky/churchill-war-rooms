Free Things to Do Near the Churchill War Rooms

No-cost attractions within walking distance of the War Rooms.

Located in the heart of London, the Churchill War Rooms are a must-visit attraction for anyone interested in history, but there's more to explore in the surrounding area without spending a penny. Within walking distance, you'll find a plethora of free attractions that complement a visit to the War Rooms, allowing you to delve deeper into the city's rich history and culture.

Whitehall and Its Landmarks

Start your exploration of the area by strolling along Whitehall, a street lined with iconic landmarks and monuments. Take in the imposing Horse Guards building, the official entrance to St James's Palace, and the Cenotaph, a memorial to the British servicemen and women who have died in combat. These sights are not only historically significant but also offer great photo opportunities.

Parks and Green Spaces

A short walk from the Churchill War Rooms, you'll find St James's Park, one of London's oldest and most beautiful royal parks. Take a leisurely stroll around the lake, visit the Duck Island Cottage, and enjoy the vibrant flora and fauna. Nearby, you'll also find Parliament Square, a tranquil oasis amidst the bustling city, and Victoria Embankment Gardens, a charming green space with beautiful gardens and a tranquil atmosphere.

Monuments and Memorials

  • The Winston Churchill Statue, a tribute to the former Prime Minister, located in Parliament Square
  • The Women's War Memorial, honouring the contributions of women during times of war
  • The Battle of Britain Monument, commemorating the bravery of the RAF during WWII

These monuments and memorials serve as poignant reminders of the area's rich history and the significant events that have shaped the nation.

Exploring the Local Area

To add context to your visit and gain a deeper understanding of the area's significance during WWII, consider joining a guided Westminster WWII walking tour. This will provide you with a unique perspective on the local landmarks and hidden gems, bringing the history to life. Alternatively, take a self-guided walk along the South Bank of the Thames, which offers stunning views of the river and the city's skyline.

Cultural Attractions

The area surrounding the Churchill War Rooms is also home to a variety of cultural attractions that are free to visit. The Westminster Abbey Museum, located within the Abbey, offers a glimpse into the history of the iconic church, while the nearby National Gallery's Sculpture Garden features an impressive collection of modern and contemporary art. Be sure to check what's on offer before you go, as some exhibitions may have limited access or require advance booking.

Practical Information

Before you start your exploration of the area, be sure to check the opening times and access restrictions for each attraction to avoid disappointment. Some areas may be closed due to events or maintenance, so it's always a good idea to confirm at checkout. With a little planning, you can make the most of your visit to the Churchill War Rooms and the surrounding area, enjoying a wealth of free attractions and experiences that will leave you with lasting memories of your time in London.
