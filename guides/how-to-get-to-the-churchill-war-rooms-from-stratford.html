Getting There

How to Get to the Churchill War Rooms from Stratford

The quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Stratford by Tube, train, bus, or on foot.

Located in the heart of London, the Churchill War Rooms are a must-visit attraction for anyone interested in history, particularly the Second World War. If you're planning to visit from Stratford, you have several options to get to this iconic destination, ranging from using the Tube to taking a stroll along the Thames. Here's a comprehensive guide to help you plan your journey.

Introduction to the Churchill War Rooms

The Churchill War Rooms are a network of underground bunkers that served as the British government's command centre during the Second World War. Led by Prime Minister Winston Churchill, the war effort was coordinated from these very rooms, making them a significant piece of history. Understanding the significance of the location can be enhanced by exploring the surrounding area, such as taking a guided Westminster WWII walking tour, which provides a broader context of the war's impact on London.

Travel by Tube

The quickest way to get to the Churchill War Rooms from Stratford is by using the London Underground, commonly known as the Tube. You can take the Central line from Stratford Station to Holborn Station, and then it's a short walk to the Churchill War Rooms. This journey is efficient and relatively quick, making it a popular choice among visitors.

  • Take the Central line from Stratford Station towards Epping.
  • Get off at Holborn Station.
  • Exit the station and follow the signs to the Churchill War Rooms.

Travel by Train

Another option is to use the national rail network. From Stratford International Station, you can take a train to London Waterloo Station, and then take the Tube (Jubilee line) to Westminster Station, which is close to the Churchill War Rooms. This option might take a bit longer than the Tube, but it's still a viable alternative.

Travel by Bus

For those who prefer to see the city above ground, taking the bus is a good option. Several bus routes connect Stratford to central London, where you can then take a shorter bus ride or walk to the Churchill War Rooms. This option is more leisurely and allows you to enjoy the city's scenery, but it may take longer due to traffic.

  • Take a bus from Stratford towards central London.
  • Change buses to one heading towards Westminster or Whitehall.
  • Get off at a stop near the Churchill War Rooms and walk to the destination.

Walking or Cycling

For the more adventurous, walking or cycling from Stratford to the Churchill War Rooms can be a great way to see the city. The journey is approximately 6-7 miles and can take a couple of hours on foot, depending on your pace. Cycling is faster and can be a fun way to explore London's bike-friendly routes. Be sure to follow traffic rules and use designated bike lanes where possible.
