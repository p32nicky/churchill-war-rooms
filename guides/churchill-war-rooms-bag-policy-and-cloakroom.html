Churchill War Rooms Bag Policy and Cloakroom

Bag size limits, security, and where to leave luggage when visiting the Churchill War Rooms.

Located in the heart of London, the Churchill War Rooms is a fascinating museum showcasing Winston Churchill's leadership during World War II. Before planning your visit, it's essential to consider the bag policy and cloakroom facilities to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

Bag Size Limits and Security

The Churchill War Rooms has strict security measures in place, including bag size limits. Visitors are advised to check the size of their bags before entering the museum, as large luggage may not be permitted. This is to ensure the safety and security of all visitors, as well as the preservation of the historic site and its artifacts.

Cloakroom Facilities

A cloakroom is available for visitors to store their luggage and personal belongings. However, space is limited, and visitors are encouraged to travel light. The cloakroom is typically located near the entrance of the museum, and staff will be happy to assist with storing and retrieving belongings.

  • Coats and umbrellas can be stored in the cloakroom
  • Large luggage and suitcases may not be accepted due to space constraints
  • Valuables such as wallets, phones, and cameras should be kept with you at all times

Alternative Luggage Storage Options

If you have large luggage or prefer not to use the cloakroom, there are alternative luggage storage options available in the surrounding area. These facilities can be used for a fee, and it's recommended to research and book in advance to ensure availability. Additionally, some nearby hotels and restaurants may offer luggage storage for their guests or customers.

Planning Your Visit

To make the most of your visit to the Churchill War Rooms, it's a good idea to plan ahead. Consider visiting the museum as part of a guided Westminster WWII walking tour, which can provide valuable context and insight into the history of the area. Be sure to check the museum's website for up-to-date information on opening times, ticket prices, and special exhibitions before you go.

Additional Tips and Reminders

When visiting the Churchill War Rooms, remember to arrive with plenty of time to spare, as security checks and cloakroom procedures can take a few minutes. It's also a good idea to wear comfortable shoes, as you'll be doing some walking during your visit. By being mindful of the bag policy and cloakroom facilities, you can focus on enjoying the museum's fascinating exhibits and learning about this important period in history.
