Getting There

How to Get to the Churchill War Rooms from Oxford

The quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Oxford by Tube, train, bus, or on foot.

The Churchill War Rooms, located in the heart of London, is a must-visit attraction for anyone interested in history, particularly the events of World War II. Situated beneath the streets of Westminster, the War Rooms offer a unique glimpse into the secret underground bunker that served as Winston Churchill's command centre during the war. If you're planning to visit from Oxford, there are several options to get to the Churchill War Rooms, each with its own advantages.

Introduction to Transport Options

Travelling from Oxford to London can be achieved by various modes of transport, including the Tube, train, bus, or even on foot for the more adventurous. The most suitable option for you will depend on your budget, time constraints, and personal preferences. It's essential to plan your journey in advance to ensure a smooth and efficient trip.

By Train

Taking the train is a convenient way to travel from Oxford to London. Regular train services operate from Oxford Station to London Paddington, from where you can take the London Underground to Westminster Station, a short walk from the Churchill War Rooms. You can also take a train to London Marylebone and then take the Bakerloo line to Westminster. Be sure to check the train schedule and plan your journey accordingly to avoid peak hours and long waits.

By Tube

Once you arrive in London, the Tube is a quick and efficient way to reach the Churchill War Rooms. The nearest Tube station is Westminster, which is served by the Jubilee, District, and Circle lines. From Oxford, you can take a train to London and then use the Tube to complete your journey. The Tube operates frequently, but it's still a good idea to check the schedule to avoid delays.

  • Take the Jubilee line from Bond Street or Baker Street to Westminster
  • Take the District line from Paddington or Victoria to Westminster
  • Take the Circle line from Victoria or Paddington to Westminster

By Bus

Another option for travelling from Oxford to the Churchill War Rooms is by bus. National Express operates coach services from Oxford to London's Victoria Coach Station, from where you can take a short bus ride or taxi to the Churchill War Rooms. This option may be more budget-friendly, but it may take longer than travelling by train or Tube.

On Foot or by Guided Tour

For those who enjoy walking or want to add some context to their visit, a guided Westminster WWII walking tour is a great way to explore the area. This tour will take you through the historic streets of Westminster, pointing out key landmarks and providing insight into the events of World War II. The tour will ultimately lead you to the Churchill War Rooms, where you can explore the underground bunker and learn more about Churchill's leadership during the war. If you prefer to walk on your own, you can take a stroll along the South Bank of the Thames, which offers beautiful views of the river and the city.
