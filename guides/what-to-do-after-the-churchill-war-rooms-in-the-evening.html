What to Do After the Churchill War Rooms in the Evening

Westminster and St James's plans for after your visit.

After immersing yourself in the historic Churchill War Rooms, you'll likely want to make the most of your evening in the Westminster area, exploring the surrounding streets and landmarks that played a significant role in Britain's wartime efforts. With its rich history, stunning architecture, and vibrant atmosphere, there's no shortage of exciting activities to enjoy in the evening.

Exploring Westminster

Westminster is steeped in history, and a stroll along Whitehall will take you past iconic landmarks such as the Cenotaph, the Horse Guards Parade, and the imposing Ministry of Defence building. As the sun sets, these buildings take on a dramatic quality, making for some fantastic photo opportunities. Be sure to walk across the road to Horse Guards Road, where you can see the Household Cavalry Mounted Regiment at the Horse Guards building.

Dining in Westminster

When it comes to dining, Westminster offers a diverse range of options to suit all tastes and budgets. From traditional British pubs serving classic fare to upscale restaurants offering fine dining experiences, you're spoiled for choice. Some popular options include the Red Lion, a historic pub that's been serving thirsty locals and visitors since 1434, and the Cinnamon Club, a renowned Indian restaurant located in the heart of Westminster.

St James's and its Attractions

A short walk from Westminster, St James's is an affluent area known for its high-end shops, art galleries, and exclusive gentlemen's clubs. In the evening, the area takes on a sophisticated ambiance, making it an ideal place to spend a relaxing evening. You can take a leisurely stroll through St James's Park, visit the prestigious Christie's auction house, or explore the historic streets and discover some of the area's unique boutiques and art galleries.

  • St James's Park, one of London's oldest and most beautiful royal parks
  • Christie's auction house, where you can attend a viewing or auction
  • Jermyn Street, famous for its high-end menswear and bespoke tailors

Adding Context with a Guided Tour

To gain a deeper understanding of the area's wartime history, consider taking a guided Westminster WWII walking tour. This will provide you with a unique perspective on the events that shaped the area during this period, including the role of the Churchill War Rooms. Your knowledgeable guide will regale you with stories and anecdotes, bringing the history to life and adding context to the sites you visit.

Evening Entertainment

In the evening, Westminster and St James's offer a variety of entertainment options, from theatre performances to live music venues. The area is home to several world-class theatres, including the Trafalgar Studios and the Playhouse Theatre, which host a range of productions throughout the year. Alternatively, you can enjoy some live music at one of the local bars or pubs, which often feature jazz and other genres.
