Getting There

How to Get to the Churchill War Rooms from Kensington

The quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Kensington by Tube, train, bus, or on foot.

The Churchill War Rooms, a fascinating museum showcasing Winston Churchill's leadership during World War II, is a must-visit attraction in London. Located in the heart of the city, it can be easily accessed from various points, including Kensington. In this article, we will explore the quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Kensington by Tube, train, bus, or on foot.

Introduction to the Churchill War Rooms

The Churchill War Rooms are situated beneath the streets of Westminster, providing a unique glimpse into the secret underground headquarters where Churchill and his government coordinated their efforts during the war. To get there from Kensington, visitors have several transportation options, each with its own advantages.

Traveling by Tube

The London Underground, commonly referred to as the Tube, is a convenient way to travel to the Churchill War Rooms from Kensington. The closest Tube station to the museum is Westminster, which is served by the Jubilee, District, and Circle lines. From Kensington, take the District line from Kensington High Street or Kensington Olympia stations, and alight at Westminster. From there, it's a short walk to the Churchill War Rooms. Alternatively, you can take the Piccadilly line from Kensington stations and change at Green Park for the Jubilee line to Westminster.

Traveling by Bus

Several bus routes connect Kensington to the Churchill War Rooms, offering a more scenic route. Visitors can take bus numbers 14, 19, 22, 24, 29, or 38 from Kensington High Street or Kensington Road, and alight at Parliament Square or Whitehall. From there, it's a short walk to the museum. Please note that bus routes and numbers are subject to change, so it's a good idea to check the latest information before your journey.

  • Bus number 14: From Kensington High Street to Parliament Square
  • Bus number 22: From Kensington Road to Whitehall
  • Bus number 24: From Kensington High Street to Parliament Square

Traveling on Foot or by Bike

For those who prefer a more leisurely pace, walking or cycling from Kensington to the Churchill War Rooms is a great option. The journey takes around 30-40 minutes on foot, depending on your pace, and offers a chance to take in the sights of London. Cyclists can use the city's bike-sharing scheme or bring their own bikes. The route takes you through some of London's most iconic streets, including Hyde Park and Whitehall. A guided Westminster WWII walking tour is a great way to add context to your visit, exploring the historical significance of the area and the events that shaped the war.

Additional Tips and Information

Before embarking on your journey to the Churchill War Rooms, it's a good idea to plan your visit in advance. Confirm the opening times and any special exhibitions or events that may be taking place during your visit. You can also book your tickets online in advance to avoid queues. Additionally, consider purchasing a London travelcard or using a contactless payment card to make your journey easier and more convenient.
