Best Pubs Near the Churchill War Rooms

Historic Westminster pubs within walking distance of the Churchill War Rooms.

After exploring the Churchill War Rooms, visitors often look to experience more of Westminster's historic charm, and what better way to do so than by visiting some of the area's most iconic pubs? These establishments have been serving locals and visitors alike for centuries, offering a glimpse into the past alongside a perfectly poured pint.

Introduction to Historic Westminster Pubs

Westminster is home to a plethora of historic pubs, each with its own unique character and stories to tell. From traditional alehouses to elegant Victorian establishments, there's something to suit every taste and preference. Many of these pubs are within walking distance of the Churchill War Rooms, making them the perfect place to stop and reflect on your visit.

Pubs Steeped in History

Some of the best pubs near the Churchill War Rooms are those that have been around since before the war. The Red Lion, located on Whitehall, is one such example. This 17th-century pub has played host to countless politicians and journalists over the years and is said to be one of the oldest pubs in Westminster. Other historic pubs in the area include:

  • The Sherlock Holmes, a Victorian-era pub located on Northumberland Street
  • The Silver Cross, a 17th-century pub on Whitehall that's said to be haunted
  • The Grenadier, a historic pub on Wilton Row that was once a favourite haunt of the Duke of Wellington

Pubs with a War-Time Connection

Given the Churchill War Rooms' focus on World War II, it's no surprise that some of the nearby pubs have their own war-time connections. The Clarence, located on Whitehall, was a popular spot for politicians and civil servants during the war, while The Old Bell on Fleet Street was a favourite haunt of journalists and reporters. A guided Westminster WWII walking tour is a great way to add context to your visit and learn more about the area's war-time history.

Modern Pubs with a Traditional Feel

While historic pubs are undoubtedly a draw, some visitors may prefer a more modern take on the traditional British pub. The Lord Moon of the Mall, located on Whitehall, offers a contemporary twist on the classic pub experience, with a wide range of beers and a menu of modern British cuisine. Other modern pubs in the area include:

  • The Westminster Arms, a pub on Storey's Gate that serves a range of real ales and delicious pub food
  • The Blue Boar, a pub on Tothill Street that offers a relaxed atmosphere and a wide range of drinks

Planning Your Visit

Before heading out to explore the pubs near the Churchill War Rooms, it's a good idea to plan your visit and check the opening hours and availability of each pub. This will help ensure that you make the most of your time in Westminster and can enjoy a pint or two in the pub of your choice.
