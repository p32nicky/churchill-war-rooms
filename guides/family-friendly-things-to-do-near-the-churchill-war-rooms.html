Family-Friendly Things to Do Near the Churchill War Rooms

Kid-pleasing stops to pair with the War Rooms.

The Churchill War Rooms, located in the heart of London, offer a fascinating glimpse into the country's rich history, particularly during World War II. While the War Rooms themselves are an absolute must-visit, there are plenty of other family-friendly attractions and activities in the surrounding area that are perfect for kids of all ages, making for a fun and educational day out in the city.

Introduction to the Local Area

The Churchill War Rooms are situated in Westminster, an area steeped in history and home to many iconic landmarks. The War Rooms are easily accessible by public transport, and once you've finished exploring the underground bunkers, you can stroll along to other nearby attractions that are sure to delight both kids and adults alike.

Museums and Galleries

London is renowned for its world-class museums, many of which are within walking distance of the Churchill War Rooms. The Imperial War Museum, for example, is a great place to learn more about British history, with a vast collection of artefacts and exhibits that bring the past to life. Other notable mentions include the National Gallery and the Tate Britain, both of which offer family-friendly activities and workshops.

  • The Imperial War Museum offers a range of interactive exhibits and displays
  • The National Gallery runs regular family events and activities
  • The Tate Britain has a dedicated family zone with hands-on activities

Parks and Green Spaces

After a morning of exploring museums and galleries, why not take a break and enjoy some fresh air in one of London's beautiful parks? St James's Park is just a short walk from the Churchill War Rooms and offers stunning views of Buckingham Palace. You can also take a leisurely boat ride on the lake or simply relax on the grass and enjoy a picnic.

Guided Tours and Historical Landmarks

To add some context to your visit to the Churchill War Rooms, consider taking a guided Westminster WWII walking tour, which will give you a deeper understanding of the area's rich history. You'll pass by iconic landmarks such as the Houses of Parliament, Big Ben, and Westminster Abbey, all of which are steeped in history and sure to captivate kids and adults alike.

Other Family-Friendly Attractions

If you're looking for something a bit different, there are plenty of other family-friendly attractions in the area that are worth visiting. The London Eye, for example, offers breathtaking views of the city, while the SEA LIFE London Aquarium is home to thousands of marine creatures from around the world. Before you head out, be sure to confirm the opening times and availability of your chosen attractions to avoid disappointment.

  • The London Eye offers stunning views of the city from its giant glass capsules
  • The SEA LIFE London Aquarium is home to a vast array of marine life
