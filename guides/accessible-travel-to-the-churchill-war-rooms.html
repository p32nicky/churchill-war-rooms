Getting There

Accessible Travel to the Churchill War Rooms

Step-free journeys and drop-off advice for the War Rooms.

The Churchill War Rooms, located in the heart of London, is a must-visit attraction for anyone interested in history, particularly the events of World War II. As one of the city's most popular destinations, it is accessible to visitors with mobility impairments, with careful planning and consideration of the surrounding area.

Getting to the Churchill War Rooms

The Churchill War Rooms are situated in Whitehall, close to several major transport hubs, including Westminster and Embankment tube stations. Both stations offer step-free access, although visitors should note that Embankment station has more straightforward lift access. It is recommended that visitors check with the transport provider for the most up-to-date information on step-free access before their visit.

Drop-off and Parking

For visitors who require a drop-off point, the Churchill War Rooms can be accessed via a designated set-down area on King Charles Street. This area is conveniently located near the entrance to the attraction and provides a safe and accessible spot for visitors to alight from their vehicle. However, please note that parking in the surrounding area is limited, and visitors are advised to plan their journey in advance, considering alternative modes of transport where possible.

  • Blue Badge holders can park in designated bays in the surrounding area, subject to availability
  • Drop-off points are clearly signposted to assist visitors with mobility impairments

Accessible Entry and Facilities

Upon arrival at the Churchill War Rooms, visitors with mobility impairments can access the attraction via a step-free entrance. The museum also offers a range of accessible facilities, including lifts, adapted toilets, and seating areas. Visitors can also borrow wheelchairs or mobility scooters, subject to availability, to assist with their visit.

Planning Your Visit

To ensure a smooth and enjoyable visit, it is recommended that visitors with mobility impairments plan their trip in advance. This includes checking the attraction's website for up-to-date information on accessibility and facilities, as well as booking any necessary assistance or equipment in advance. Visitors may also consider combining their visit with a guided Westminster WWII walking tour, which can provide valuable context and insights into the history of the area.

Additional Accessibility Information

The Churchill War Rooms are committed to providing an accessible and enjoyable experience for all visitors. In addition to the facilities and services mentioned above, the attraction also offers audio descriptions, touch tours, and British Sign Language interpretation, subject to availability. Visitors are advised to contact the attraction in advance to discuss their specific needs and requirements, ensuring that they can make the most of their visit.

  • Audio descriptions and touch tours can be booked in advance, subject to availability
  • British Sign Language interpretation is available for pre-booked groups and events
