Getting There

How to Get to the Churchill War Rooms from Covent Garden

The quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Covent Garden by Tube, train, bus, or on foot.

The Churchill War Rooms, a fascinating museum showcasing Winston Churchill's leadership during World War II, is a must-visit attraction in London. Located in the heart of the city, it is easily accessible from Covent Garden, a popular spot for shopping, dining, and entertainment. Here's a guide on how to get to the Churchill War Rooms from Covent Garden using various modes of transportation.

By Tube

The quickest way to reach the Churchill War Rooms from Covent Garden is by taking the Tube. The nearest Tube station to the Churchill War Rooms is Westminster, which is served by the Jubilee, District, and Circle lines. From Covent Garden, take the Piccadilly line from Covent Garden Station to Green Park, then change to the Jubilee line and travel to Westminster. The journey takes around 10-15 minutes.

By Train

Alternatively, you can take a train from Covent Garden to the Churchill War Rooms. The nearest train station is Waterloo, which is a short walk from the Churchill War Rooms. From Covent Garden, take the Tube to Waterloo Station, or take a taxi or bus. However, please note that taking the train is not the most direct or efficient way to reach the Churchill War Rooms from Covent Garden.

By Bus

Taking the bus is another option to reach the Churchill War Rooms from Covent Garden. You can take bus numbers 6, 9, 11, 24, or 87 from Covent Garden to Westminster. The journey takes around 20-30 minutes, depending on traffic. Here are some bus routes you can take:

  • Bus number 6: From Aldwych to Whitehall
  • Bus number 9: From Aldwych to Whitehall
  • Bus number 11: From Covent Garden to Westminster

On Foot

Walking is a great way to reach the Churchill War Rooms from Covent Garden, especially if you want to take in the sights and sounds of the city. The journey takes around 20-30 minutes, depending on your pace. You can walk along the South Bank of the Thames, taking in the views of the river and the city. Alternatively, you can walk through the streets of Westminster, passing by iconic landmarks such as Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament.

Adding Context with a Guided Tour

A great way to add context to your visit to the Churchill War Rooms is to take a guided Westminster WWII walking tour. This will give you a deeper understanding of the history and significance of the Churchill War Rooms, as well as other important sites in the area. The tour will take you through the streets of Westminster, pointing out key locations and landmarks, and providing insight into the events and decisions that shaped the course of the war.

Planning Your Visit

Before you set out to the Churchill War Rooms, make sure to plan your visit in advance. Check the attraction's website to confirm opening hours and ticket availability. It's also a good idea to book your tickets in advance to avoid queues and ensure entry. With its rich history and fascinating exhibits, the Churchill War Rooms is a must-visit attraction in London, and with these transportation options, you can easily get there from Covent Garden.
