Getting There

How to Get to the Churchill War Rooms from Heathrow Airport

The quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Heathrow Airport by Tube, train, bus, or on foot.

Located in the heart of London, the Churchill War Rooms are a must-visit attraction for anyone interested in history, particularly the events of World War II. Situated beneath the streets of Westminster, the War Rooms are easily accessible from Heathrow Airport, with a range of transport options available to suit all budgets and preferences.

Introduction to Transport Options

The quickest and most convenient way to reach the Churchill War Rooms from Heathrow Airport is by Tube, which takes approximately 45-60 minutes. However, there are also options to take the train, bus, or even a taxi, depending on your budget and the amount of time you have available. It is always a good idea to confirm the latest transport information and to plan your journey in advance to ensure a smooth and hassle-free trip.

Tube Journey from Heathrow Airport

The Tube is a fast and efficient way to reach the Churchill War Rooms from Heathrow Airport. To take the Tube, follow the signs at the airport to the London Underground station, which is located near the terminals. Take the Piccadilly line from Heathrow to Green Park, then change to the Jubilee line and travel to Westminster, which is the closest station to the Churchill War Rooms. The journey takes around 45-60 minutes, depending on the time of day and the number of changes.

  • Take the Piccadilly line from Heathrow to Green Park
  • Change to the Jubilee line at Green Park
  • Travel on the Jubilee line to Westminster

Train and Bus Options

Alternatively, you can take the train or bus from Heathrow Airport to central London. The train is a quick option, taking around 15-30 minutes to reach Paddington station, from where you can take the Tube or a taxi to the Churchill War Rooms. The bus is a more affordable option, but it takes longer, around 60-90 minutes depending on traffic. There are also coach services available, such as National Express, which operate from Heathrow Airport to Victoria Coach Station, which is a short walk or Tube ride from the Churchill War Rooms.

Walking and Guided Tours

For those who prefer a more leisurely approach, it is possible to walk from Westminster station to the Churchill War Rooms, which takes around 5-10 minutes. A guided Westminster WWII walking tour is a great way to add context to your visit, taking in other notable landmarks and sites of historical interest along the way. This can be a great way to learn more about the history of the area and the significance of the Churchill War Rooms.

On Foot and by Taxi

While it is not recommended to attempt to walk from Heathrow Airport to the Churchill War Rooms, as it is a long distance, around 17 miles, it is possible to take a taxi. This is the most expensive option, but it can be convenient if you have a lot of luggage or prefer a door-to-door service. The journey takes around 60-90 minutes depending on traffic, and it is always a good idea to confirm the price with the driver before you start your journey.
