Churchill's Bodyguard: Walter Thompson

The detective who protected Churchill for years.

As one of the most iconic leaders in British history, Winston Churchill's safety was of paramount importance, particularly during the tumultuous years of World War II. For nearly 20 years, Detective Inspector Walter Thompson served as Churchill's loyal bodyguard, protecting him from potential threats and becoming an integral part of the former Prime Minister's daily life.

Early Life and Career

Walter Thompson was born in 1890 and joined the Metropolitan Police Service in 1913. He worked his way up the ranks, earning a reputation as a skilled and dedicated officer. In 1920, Thompson was assigned to the Special Branch, where he would eventually be tasked with protecting high-profile figures, including royalty and government officials.

Assignment to Churchill

In 1921, Thompson was assigned to serve as the personal bodyguard to Winston Churchill, who was then the Secretary of State for the Colonies. This marked the beginning of a long and storied relationship between the two men, with Thompson providing protection and support to Churchill throughout his career. As Churchill's bodyguard, Thompson was responsible for ensuring his safety at all times, whether he was attending official events or simply going about his daily routine.

Protecting Churchill During WWII

During World War II, Thompson's role as Churchill's bodyguard became even more critical. With the threat of Nazi espionage and assassination plots looming large, Thompson was constantly on the lookout for potential dangers. Some of the key challenges he faced included:

  • Protecting Churchill during his frequent visits to the front lines and military bases
  • Ensuring the Prime Minister's safety during meetings with other world leaders, such as President Franklin D. Roosevelt
  • Screening mail and packages for potential threats, such as bombs or poison
Despite these challenges, Thompson proved himself to be a skilled and dedicated bodyguard, earning Churchill's trust and respect.

Life with Churchill

As Churchill's bodyguard, Thompson was privy to many aspects of the former Prime Minister's personal life. He often accompanied Churchill on his travels, both within the UK and abroad, and was familiar with his daily routines and habits. Thompson also developed a deep understanding of Churchill's personality and character, allowing him to anticipate and respond to potential threats. For those looking to gain a deeper understanding of Churchill's life and experiences during WWII, a guided Westminster WWII walking tour can provide valuable context and insights.

Legacy and Later Life

After the war, Thompson continued to serve as Churchill's bodyguard until his retirement in 1945. He was awarded the King's Police Medal for his service and later wrote a memoir about his experiences as Churchill's bodyguard. Today, Thompson's legacy serves as a testament to the important role that bodyguards and security personnel play in protecting high-profile figures and ensuring their safety. His story is a fascinating glimpse into the life and times of one of Britain's most iconic leaders, and a reminder of the bravery and dedication of those who serve behind the scenes.
