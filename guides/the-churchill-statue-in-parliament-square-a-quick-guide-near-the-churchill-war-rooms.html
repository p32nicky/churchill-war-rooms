the Churchill Statue in Parliament Square: A Quick Guide Near the Churchill War Rooms

What to know about the Churchill Statue in Parliament Square, a short walk from the Churchill War Rooms, and how to fit it into your day.

A short walk from the Churchill War Rooms, the Churchill Statue in Parliament Square is a must-visit attraction for anyone interested in the life and legacy of Winston Churchill. Located in the heart of London, this iconic statue is a great addition to a day out exploring the city's rich history and heritage.

History of the Churchill Statue

The Churchill Statue was designed by Ivor Roberts-Jones and unveiled in 1973 by Lord Mountbatten. The statue depicts Churchill standing in a typical pose, with his feet shoulder-width apart and his hands on his hips. It is made from bronze and stands over 12 feet tall, making it an imposing figure in the square.

Location and Accessibility

The Churchill Statue is located in Parliament Square, which is easily accessible by public transport. The nearest tube stations are Westminster and St James's Park, both of which are a short walk from the square. Visitors can also take a bus or taxi to the area. The statue is situated in a pedestrianised area, making it easy to view and take photos.

Things to Do and See

There are plenty of things to do and see in and around Parliament Square. Some of the top attractions include:

  • Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament
  • The Supreme Court
  • Westminster Abbey
  • Other statues of famous historical figures, including Nelson Mandela and Mahatma Gandhi

Visitors can also take a stroll along Whitehall, which is home to many other famous landmarks and attractions.

Adding Context to Your Visit

To get a deeper understanding of the history and significance of the Churchill Statue, consider visiting as part of a guided Westminster WWII walking tour. This will provide context and insights into the life and times of Churchill, as well as the impact of the war on the city and its people.

Planning Your Day

To make the most of your visit to the Churchill Statue, it's a good idea to plan your day in advance. This might involve booking a timed-entry ticket to the Churchill War Rooms, and then allowing some time to walk to Parliament Square and take in the sights. Be sure to check the opening times and ticket availability for the War Rooms before you go, and confirm at checkout.
