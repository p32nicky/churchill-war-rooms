Churchill War Rooms Accessibility Guide for Wheelchair Users

Step-free access, lifts, and what to expect at the Churchill War Rooms if you use a wheelchair.

The Churchill War Rooms, located in the heart of London, offer a unique glimpse into the British government's wartime operations during World War II. As a wheelchair user, you can enjoy step-free access to this historic site, exploring the very rooms where Winston Churchill and his team coordinated the British war effort.

Arrival and Entrance

Upon arrival, you'll find the main entrance to the Churchill War Rooms is wheelchair accessible, with a gentle slope leading to the door. The entrance is located on King Charles Street, just off Whitehall. Once inside, you'll find a spacious foyer with plenty of room to manoeuvre.

Getting Around the Museum

The Churchill War Rooms are largely self-contained, with the majority of the exhibits located on a single level. A network of lifts connects the various areas of the museum, ensuring that all exhibits are accessible to wheelchair users. You can move freely between the different sections, including the Cabinet Room, the Map Room, and the Churchill Museum.

  • Lifts are available to access all levels of the museum
  • Wide, wheelchair-accessible corridors connect the different exhibit areas
  • Seating areas are provided throughout the museum for visitors who need to rest

Exhibits and Interactive Displays

The Churchill War Rooms feature a wide range of exhibits and interactive displays, all of which are wheelchair accessible. You can explore the very rooms where Churchill and his team worked, see authentic wartime artefacts, and learn about the history of the British war effort through interactive displays and films.

Additional Facilities and Services

In addition to the exhibits, the Churchill War Rooms offer a range of facilities and services to support visitors with disabilities. These include accessible toilets, a wheelchair-accessible café, and audio descriptions and touch tours for visitors with visual impairments. It's a good idea to confirm the availability of these services when you plan your visit.

  • Accessible toilets are located on each level of the museum
  • The café is wheelchair accessible and offers a range of refreshments
  • Audio descriptions and touch tours can be arranged in advance

Planning Your Visit

To make the most of your visit to the Churchill War Rooms, it's a good idea to plan ahead. You can book your tickets online and confirm the accessibility features that are important to you. Consider combining your visit with a guided Westminster WWII walking tour, which can provide valuable context and insights into the history of the area. By planning your visit carefully, you can ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at this unique and fascinating historic site.
