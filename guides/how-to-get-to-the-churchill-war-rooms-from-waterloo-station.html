Getting There

How to Get to the Churchill War Rooms from Waterloo Station

The quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Waterloo Station by Tube, train, bus, or on foot.

The Churchill War Rooms, a museum showcasing Winston Churchill's leadership during World War II, is a must-visit attraction in London. Located in the heart of the city, it is easily accessible from Waterloo Station, a major transportation hub. In this article, we will explore the quickest routes to the Churchill War Rooms from Waterloo Station by various modes of transport.

Introduction to the Churchill War Rooms

The Churchill War Rooms are situated beneath the streets of Westminster, offering a unique glimpse into the British government's operations during World War II. To plan your visit, it is essential to know the best ways to get to the museum from Waterloo Station.

By Tube

The quickest way to reach the Churchill War Rooms from Waterloo Station is by taking the London Underground, also known as the Tube. From Waterloo Station, take the Jubilee Line to Westminster Station, which is a short walk from the Churchill War Rooms. You can also take the Bakerloo Line from Waterloo to Embankment Station and then walk to the museum.

  • Take the Jubilee Line from Waterloo Station to Westminster Station
  • Alternatively, take the Bakerloo Line from Waterloo to Embankment Station

By Bus

Another option is to take the bus from Waterloo Station to the Churchill War Rooms. Several bus routes operate from Waterloo to Westminster, including the RV1 and 381 buses. You can also take the 3, 12, or 53 buses from Waterloo Road to Whitehall, which is a short walk from the museum.

  • Take the RV1 or 381 bus from Waterloo Station to Westminster
  • Alternatively, take the 3, 12, or 53 bus from Waterloo Road to Whitehall

On Foot

If you prefer to walk, the Churchill War Rooms are approximately a 20-25 minute walk from Waterloo Station. This route takes you along the South Bank of the Thames, offering stunning views of the river and the city. You can also take a guided Westminster WWII walking tour, which provides a great way to add context to your visit and learn more about the history of the area.

By Train and Other Options

While the Tube and bus are the most convenient options, you can also take a train from Waterloo Station to London Bridge Station and then take the Tube or bus to the Churchill War Rooms. Additionally, you can take a taxi or ride-hailing service from Waterloo Station, but this is likely to be more expensive than public transport.

  • Take a train from Waterloo Station to London Bridge Station and then the Tube or bus
  • Alternatively, take a taxi or ride-hailing service from Waterloo Station

Planning Your Visit

Before you set out, be sure to check the Churchill War Rooms' website for up-to-date information on opening times and ticket availability. It is also a good idea to plan your route in advance and confirm the best option for your travel style and budget. With its rich history and convenient location, the Churchill War Rooms are a must-visit destination in London, and with these transport options, you can easily get there from Waterloo Station.
