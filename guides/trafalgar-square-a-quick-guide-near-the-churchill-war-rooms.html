Trafalgar Square: A Quick Guide Near the Churchill War Rooms

What to know about Trafalgar Square, a short walk from the Churchill War Rooms, and how to fit it into your day.

Trafalgar Square is one of London's most iconic public spaces, offering a wealth of history, culture, and entertainment, all within a short walk from the Churchill War Rooms, making it an ideal addition to your day out in the city.

Introduction to Trafalgar Square

Trafalgar Square is a vibrant and historic square in the heart of London, named after the Battle of Trafalgar, a British naval victory in 1805. The square is home to the iconic Lord Nelson's column, which stands tall in the centre, surrounded by four large lions and a number of important buildings, including the National Gallery and St Martin-in-the-Fields church.

Key Attractions and Landmarks

Visitors to Trafalgar Square can explore a range of attractions, including the National Gallery, which houses a world-class collection of Western European art, featuring works by artists such as Van Gogh, Monet, and Leonardo da Vinci. The square is also home to a number of statues and monuments, including those dedicated to Lord Nelson and other notable figures from British history.

  • The National Gallery, with its impressive collection of art
  • St Martin-in-the-Fields church, a beautiful and historic place of worship
  • The imposing Lord Nelson's column, a iconic symbol of the square

Things to Do and See

As well as exploring the many attractions and landmarks, visitors to Trafalgar Square can enjoy a range of activities, including street performances, public events, and exhibitions. The square is also a great place to people watch, with its lively atmosphere and diverse range of visitors. For those looking to add some context to their visit, a guided Westminster WWII walking tour is a great way to learn more about the area's rich history, including its significance during the war.

Eating and Drinking

There are plenty of options for eating and drinking in and around Trafalgar Square, from traditional British pubs to cafes and restaurants serving a range of international cuisine. Visitors can grab a quick snack or meal from one of the many food outlets, or sit down to a more leisurely lunch or dinner at one of the square's many restaurants.

  • Traditional British pubs, serving a range of beers and pub food
  • Cafes, offering coffee, cakes, and light meals
  • Restaurants, serving a diverse range of international cuisine

Getting There and Around

Trafalgar Square is easily accessible by public transport, with several nearby tube stations, including Charing Cross and Leicester Square. The square is also within walking distance of a number of other popular London attractions, including the Churchill War Rooms, making it a great place to visit as part of a day out in the city. Visitors can plan their route and check for up-to-date travel information before they go, to ensure a smooth and stress-free journey.

Planning Your Visit

Before visiting Trafalgar Square, it's a good idea to check opening times and plan your day out in advance. This will help you make the most of your time and ensure that you don't miss out on any of the square's many attractions and activities.
